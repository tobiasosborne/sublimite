**Pick: simd-filter-verify** (SIMD filter + verify with a deterministic Two-Way fallback after bounded verification work). It is 2.5–5x faster on ordinary finds and 10–35x faster on the adversarial G6v rows; Two-Way-only has no filter, so every G6v row costs a full verified scan. Not a Pareto question: simd dominates on every row except G6_newline p99 in run 1. Under load neither meets G6; per the policy that is a note.

Landed: the simd variant's `src/find/*` (literal.c/h, find_int.h, find.c changes), `tests/find_simd_filter_verify_test.c`, `docs/decisions/P1.10-simd-filter-verify.md`. The twoway-only branch (`wt/edit-4w1.10-twoway-only`, decision record `docs/decisions/P1.10-twoway-only.md` there) is kept for the best-of step: its tests and edge cases are folded into src/find by a follow-up synthesis bead, then the branch is deleted.
