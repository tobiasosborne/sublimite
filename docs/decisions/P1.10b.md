Fixture hygiene: the losing snapshot planting expression could underflow when
the needle exceeded its tiny haystack. The port guards `needle_length <= length`
before planting. Snapshot builders release the temporary tree on every error,
and snapshots outlive tree destruction. RNG and oracle/result storage are local;
the ports introduce no mutable globals. Compile/scratch storage is explicitly
aligned and bounded by the frozen public maxima.

## Red-green and implementation decision

The regression compiles the real `literal.c` in the test translation unit with
external symbols renamed and only reader iterator/poll callbacks instrumented.
Local probe storage travels through the snapshot source's ignored `bytes` field
and the synthetic work context's `arg`. Normal linked production entry points
also run every functional fixture. There are no shipped hooks or clock-based
test assertions.

RED ran before source edits with Clang ASan/UBSan and leak detection disabled.
Launch stamp: `Not charging`, (M)[AC], one-minute load 11.96. Exit 1:

```text
snapshot traversal seeks=4608 span_next=4608 bound_seeks=3 bound_spans=3072
FAIL tests/find_bestof_test.c:322: probe.seeks<=3 && probe.spans>0 && probe.spans<=3*spans
FAIL tests/find_bestof_test.c:384: snapshot_traversal(&t)==0
```

The traversal counts are (M)[AC], load 11.96; bounds are regression gates (G).
The old lazy reader reset its cached base to each requested offset. A backwards
prefix therefore re-seeked for every byte, even within the same physical span,
and span transitions also re-seeked instead of advancing the iterator. This
lost deterministic linear span traversal and omitted metering that traversal.

`find_int.h` adds a snapshot-only forward reader that seeks once, advances its
iterator through intervening spans, and meters each initial/next span fetch.
`literal.c` supplies independent suffix and prefix readers to long snapshot
Two-Way. Prefix comparisons now proceed in ascending order, also in the flat
specialization, preserving the same shift decisions and final retirement poll.

Why the cursors are monotone: a right-half mismatch retires through its failed
byte. After a complete right half, periodic memory or the nonperiodic shift
moves the next suffix comparison beyond it. The selected critical cut precedes
the true period; a nonperiodic shift exceeds the prefix length. Thus a failed
prefix also cannot make its cursor move backwards. Each cursor traverses each
span at most once in one seek, with constant stack space and no allocation.
The picked kernel needs two cursors, since it has no losing-variant tail table.
The test deliberately retains the losing design's looser three-pass bound.
