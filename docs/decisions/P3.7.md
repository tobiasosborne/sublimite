## Design and measurement boundaries

All variants use `bench/prewake_bench.c`; `tools/prewake_bench.sh` builds them
without changing the Makefile. Implementations live in `variants/P3.7/a`, `b`,
and `c`, with a common caller-owned state/API. The renderer's production source
is privately included with renamed exports, just as its existing readback tests
do. No production source, frozen header, new global, or Makefile was changed.

| Variant | Eligible hint action | Additional policy |
|---|---|---|
| A | Nothing | Baseline; hinted A includes the same scheduled-key wait and instrumentation |
| B | Bind existing render state, submit a zero-instance GL draw, flush and wait for its fence, spin UI for 200 us (E, configured) | Focus-in, pointer motion, or non-repeated modifier key-down after >=15 s (G, workload); suppress further warm-ups within that interval |
| C | B, then synchronous busy-wait for another 100 ms (E, configured) | Implements the bead's busy-wait alternative; no sched_setattr/uclamp permission dependency |

The supported modifier set is Shift, Control, Alt, Super and ISO Level3 Shift,
on either side where applicable. Printable keys, releases, focus-out and repeats
do not hint. The caller records typing activity with `prewake_activity`.
Errors return codes, and a failed warm-up never proceeds into the spin.
The contract test exercises all trigger classes, threshold, debounce, invalid
arguments, warm-up failure, and exact requested spin budgets.

The common bench creates native windows **only on DISPLAY=:99 EDIT_DISPLAY=:99**
and refuses any other display or real-display opt-in. EGL reports **llvmpipe**;
this is CPU software rendering. It uses the production EGL context/resources,
`gl_submit`, upload, draw, swap and fence code. The production DRI3 Present
verification is not replaced: the private benchmark times swap/fence without
claiming a displayed completion, vblank or optical endpoint. Context/resources
are allocated on an init worker. Frames use fixed storage and assert no counted
allocations through snapshot/submit; native present and fence operations are
outside that guard, as in the settled renderer policy.

Surface: 2880x1800 pixels (E, configured); whole cells occupy the actual native
window recorded in READY. A cached ASCII atlas and 43200 cells (M, READY stamp)
are fully drawn; the first cell background changes on each frame. Three init
frames (E, configured) remove shader-JIT/startup effects before idle trials.
The measured frame starts immediately before snapshot/submit and ends after the
post-swap fence. It excludes mutation/layout/minimap, so this is a **G3i-style
proxy**, not the end-to-end editor/optical G2b gate.

The hint precedes a scheduled synthetic key by 50 ms (E, configured). `frame_ns`
measures work after the UI starts that frame; `key_ns` additionally includes
sleep overshoot or the busy-wait delaying that key. This prevents C from hiding
its blocking cost outside the first-key latency. A/N and A/H both do no warm-up,
so A/H controls the scheduling/instrumentation and time trend of the hint rows.
A zero-instance draw may be elided by a native driver; its fence alone is not
proof that a real GPU left a low-power state.

The single full run uses 200 trials per variant **per condition** (E, requested),
serially ordered A/N, B/N, C/N, A/H, B/H, C/H, repeated. A fresh 15-second sleep
(E, configured) precedes every sample; no other benchmark variant runs during
that sleep. Each renderer is persistent and has at least that much inactivity.
The box remains shared; physical CPU idleness is neither asserted nor sought.
Before every sample, the runner reads BAT0 status and the first loadavg field;
the executable reads them again before timing. Every raw sample carries power
and load. Full make/fuzz/sanitizer validation overlaps early samples and is part
of the reported shared load. No trials are filtered or re-run for a quieter box.

Percentiles use nearest rank. Raw evidence is `variants/P3.7/evidence/trials.log`;
`variants/P3.7/summarize.py` verifies ordering, exact counts, idle durations and
allocation checks before producing the final table. Page-fault samples are
retained and cumulative major fault counters are reported, not filtered. All results are TRACK.
G3i target A is 8 / 11.1 ms (G); the coordinator owns any gate verdict.

## CPU and energy interpretation

`hint_cpu_ns` is CLOCK_PROCESS_CPUTIME_ID around the complete hint, summing all
app threads including llvmpipe; `hint_wall_ns` is its wall time. The spin expires
by wall clock, so shared-box descheduling can reduce CPU consumption without
reducing its input-blocking time. A/H's empty call has instrumentation overhead;
its tiny measured CPU is not an implementation cost.

G11's idle CPU target is 0.1 / 0.2 ms per blink (G), with <=2 wakeups/s blinking
and zero after idle timeout/unfocus (G). Hint CPU is reported alongside that
budget for scale, **not substituted for a measured blink**. This experiment
adds no periodic idle wake-up: it responds only to an eligible event. False
positive hints incur the full warm-up/spin cost even when no key follows.
C's synchronous spin spends its complete budget whether a key follows or not.
A future production evaluation must measure real blink CPU plus hints and count
false-positive events under actual pointer use.

Package RAPL `/sys/class/powercap/intel-rapl:0/energy_uj` is unreadable
(permission denied); BAT0 reports Not charging and `power_now` reports zero
at the probe ((M)[AC], BAT0=Not charging, load1=6.37), so it cannot measure short hint energy. **Joules are unavailable**;
CPU time is an explicitly labelled cost proxy, not energy. No root access or
privilege escalation for energy counters was attempted.

Cold-cache rows are **SKIP**: drop_caches requires root, which the brief excludes.
No cache flush, corpus regeneration, or large fixture was created.

## Pareto front and recommendation

Both the raw numerical front (including max RSS) and the decision front
(first-key p50/p99, hint CPU p50/p99 and full ELF size) will be reported. The
render reservations and hint storage layout are identical across variants;
small process RSS differences include driver/loader/startup effects and are
not a different memory design. RSS stays in the evidence table and raw front,
while the decision front uses the controlled latency/CPU/size axes.

To be completed from the validated full-run table. A GPU policy cannot be
adopted from Xvfb's software-renderer timings alone. The busy-wait alternative
also has a deterministic key-delay risk: with the configured key lead, its
100.2 ms (E) spin can hold a key for at least 50.2 ms (E), even with instantaneous
GPU work. A nonblocking, bounded sched_setattr/uclamp implementation is a separate
alternative and is not measured by this C variant.

## What a real-display run must establish

Only the coordinator arranges and announces such a run to Tobias. This worker
never launches a window outside :99. The present scripts deliberately cannot
run on a real display without a separately reviewed guard change.

Xvfb cannot show real GPU RC6/device power-state exit, display PSR, real scanout,
real vblank alignment/slips, compositor/panel wake, or optical G2b latency.
Shared Xvfb/llvmpipe results also do not isolate CPU C-state exit; unrelated
workers, X-server work and the required pre-sample stamps can wake shared cores.

The later run needs the actual A panel/resolution/refresh and production DRI3
GL adapter with real matching PIXMAP completion. Confirm the renderer is the
hardware device, then record T0/input, submit, swap, device fence, matching
Present/vblank, and optical latency where available. Keep the interleaved
200-trial per condition protocol (E, requested), >=15 s app idle (G, workload),
power/load stamps, and shared-box measurement policy. Sweep hint-to-key lead
(including a modifier pressed immediately with a printable key), record focus,
motion and modifier false positives, and measure the no-following-key cost.
Record CPU/GPU C-state residency and readable package/GPU energy counters with
wrap handling and a background baseline; process CPU alone cannot establish
energy. Recheck G11 blink CPU/wakeups with hints enabled, debounce pointer storms,
and ensure a no-op command/fence actually wakes the intended hardware path.
Do not fabricate T6 on an unsupported backend. Cold-cache remains skipped unless
the coordinator separately authorizes the root operation.
