# Wave 2 harvest — platform, rendering and view

This dossier records source evidence for the Wave 2 derivations in PLAN_V2 §§4.5–4.6. Function names below identify v1 behavior to preserve or account for. They do not prescribe v2 headers.

**Reading key.** Oracle paths below are relative to `/home/tobias/Projects/sublimite-v1-oracle`. The recorded pin is `6fec2e8` (v2 `docs/decisions/wave0-beads-and-oracle-pin.md`); PLAN's older line locators are identified here by file and symbol. Counts marked **(M)** are static source measurements: per named file, `gcc -x c -fpreprocessed -dD -E -P "$f" | grep -c '[^[:space:]]'`. Explicit C input applies the same method to headers and includes. This retains conditional branches and directives, strips comments/blanks and does not expand includes. Power and load do not affect source counts. Tables are disjoint inventories of named donor files, including headers and whole mixed-purpose files, not estimates of code to port. Generated Wayland protocol outputs under `wayland/protocol/`, unrelated editor code and test/bench sources are outside the totals. Embedded font tables are explicitly itemized. Names such as Q8, T5/T6, X11, GL 3.3, section labels and finding numbers are identifiers, formats or selectors from the cited sources. A bound marked (G) comes from its named rule. Historical timings retain their original conditions. No new runtime measurements were taken.

**Layer placement.** PLAN §2 puts platform and rendering in L3 and viewport/layout/tabs/minimap in L4. `app_paint` belongs to L5 even though the Wave 2 delivery list groups it with the UI work. PLAN §2's module order includes `viewport < layout < tabs` and separate raster/GL consumers of render and native-platform surfaces. The Wave 1 piece-lines decision and amendment A19 retire standalone `lineidx`; wherever older §4.5 prose says `piece/lineidx`, use the signed piece line queries.

**Boundary and defects.** `x11/clip.c` includes `wayland/private.h` and `wayland/dispatch.c` includes `x11/private.h`, `x11/dnd.h` and `x11/clip.h`. The public zero-copy dispatch even takes an `x11_clip_buf *`. This is the private-header cycle forbidden by CLAUDE.md. A backend-state cast requires a backend discriminator: the pin's `plat_clip_set_buf` does dispatch on it, and the pin decision records the earlier Wayland copy failure as fixed in a mock. Reopening that cast or borrowing another backend's private struct defeats both the lifetime rule and the layer check. Keep the real copy/paste gate. `x11_dnd_*` queues a payload-free DROP and retrieves a shared last result, so a later completed drop can replace bytes before delivery. Wayland `device_drop` calls `device_leave` and its `plat_drop_data` branch returns NULL. `push` drops the newest event on saturation. `clip_receive`/`clip_send` service nonblocking fds but a peer may never finish. Preserve terminal control and reclaim transfer resources on stall, focus/seat loss where applicable, and teardown. Keep generated protocol inputs reproducible from committed XML under the PLAN check. Wayland wl_shm and Win32 are excluded by Law 5. The X11 connection ownership constraint from finding 8 applies here and at raster submit.

**Port witnesses.** `x11_input_test`, `x11_order_test`, `x11_xi2_test`, `x11_clip_test`, `x11_clip_review_test`, `x11_dnd_test`, `x11_activate_test`, `x11_live_test`, `x11_stall_test`, `window_size_test` and `x11_font_size_test` exercise translation, reordering, transfer, activation and sizing. `wayland_test` with `wayland_mock.h`, `wayland_startup_test` and `wayland_gl_test` exercise compositor lifecycle; keep the real compositor run as separate evidence. **Q1 (§4.6). How does queue admission preserve key order and terminal control under saturation?** Evidence: `plat_event` is already atomic, while Wayland `push` can discard a transition. Options: reserve terminal capacity with bounded producer backpressure, or keep sequenced pending terminal state outside the input ring and merge by order. Exercise down/repeat/focus-out/up, configure and close with a full ring.

**Q2 (§4.6). When does transfer ownership change, and what bounds a stalled peer?** Evidence: asynchronous X ownership checks and Wayland send fds survive the initiating call, while Xdnd fetches the last drop. Options: move a prepaid buffer into each accepted event, or retain a generation-checked buffer slot until acknowledgement. For either, compare an absolute lifetime with a progress deadline plus an absolute cap; distinguish transfer failure from ownership loss.

**Q3 (§4.6). Which surface changes can coalesce without crossing an input coordinate or frame boundary?** Evidence: `fraction_preferred` and xdg configure update different inputs to buffer size. Options: queue a coherent surface snapshot before dependent input, or stamp input/frames with a retained geometry generation; compare fractional-scale-only transitions and rapid configure sequences.

## render and render_submit_prep — one frame contract

| Oracle directory under `src/` | File and comment-free lines (M) |
|---|---|
| `render/` | `render.c` 468, `render.h` 160, `render_null.c` 45 |

**Source footprint (M) 673 lines**, the sum of the file counts above.

**Behavior and functions.** `render_grid_init` binds caller-owned cells and a dirty-row bitmap. `render_frame_begin`, `render_mark_rows`, `render_row_dirty`, `render_mark_full` and `render_dirty_strips` preserve retained cells and describe exact contiguous dirty runs, including empty damage. `render_grid_validate` checks extents, strides, glyph rectangles, paired wide cells, the scrolling overscan and the independently positioned RGB region. Keep the 20-byte pointer-free cell (P: four uint32_t plus two uint16_t fields, verified by the static assertion in `render.h`). Slots identify glyph rectangles. The app resolves selection/cursor colors before submit.

`render_null_backend` is the headless witness. `render_backend_query/init/resize/check/submit/present/event/signal/stats/shutdown` (all with prefix `render_backend_`) establish admission, lifetime and error behavior. Init may block on the worker. Resize while idle requires a full next frame. Submit must validate canonical strips and capacity before consuming an ID. On success callers can reuse cells, hit-independent metadata and RGB region storage immediately. Referenced atlas pixels remain immutable through T5 or quiescent shutdown. BUSY allows coalescing and retry. `render_deliver`, `render_trace_device_done` and `render_trace_present_complete` preserve original IDs and monotonic stamps even when notifications arrive early or out of order. Depth one (G, PLAN §4.6: the active slot stays owned until device and presentation retirement). T4 is successful present submission. T5 cannot precede T4. T6 is observed matching presentation. Lost feedback requires quiescent restart, and a discarding compositor cannot produce a displayed-frame time.

**Shared preparation to derive.** There is no v1 `render_submit_prep` file. The concrete donors are `render_backend_check` and `render_dirty_strips` here, `cpu_submit` in raster, and `gl_submit` in GL. CPU copies canonical strips, glyph/page descriptors, overscan and the independently damaged region into prepaid storage. GL separately counts referenced glyph pixels, computes tight page offsets, accounts for padded source stride, detects changed atlas rows and flips region scanlines for its texture. A valid padded R8 source must sample identically through both backends. Check all capacities before publication, including overscan, physical client pixels, unique glyph rectangles, table bytes and minimap region. Keep backend-specific transfer and Y inversion downstream. A rejected frame must leave retryable input and no dangling work or pins.

**Boundary and defects.** Retire `render_grid_repair` and the app's retry-after-repair path. Replacing an invalid descriptor with a blank disguises the producing error and can alter retained rows. Keep explicit validation before submit and propagate the reason. Correlated flags inside `render_backend` currently encode delivery, device signal, presentation and full-frame requirements. Derive lifecycle states that can represent discard without inventing T6. Independently chosen frame limits and fractional rendering in CPU and GL make successful admission or displayed pixels backend-dependent; `render_grid_validate` alone does not establish parity. Finding 8 reaches `render_backend_submit` via a backend callback. The live guard covers the entire call.

**Port witnesses.** `render_test`, `render_fuzz` and `render_bench` cover grid validation, canonical damage, stale/duplicate completion and headless timing. Cross-check actual backend output with `gl_scroll_test`, `raster_scroll_test` and `unicode_render_test`; include zero damage, padded pages, final overscan row, wide glyphs, region-only damage and capacity refusal.

**Q4 (§4.6). Which admission and packing work is reusable without retaining mutable caller metadata?** Evidence: CPU borrows R8 pages through T5; GL repacks referenced rectangles, while both snapshot region and cell damage. Options: produce a checked descriptor/offset plan with immutable source-page leases, or pack referenced coverage into prepaid frame-owned storage once. Compare sparse damage and full high-DPI frames, including overflow and retry before publication.

**Q5 (§4.6). What pixel rule and readback prove fractional-scroll parity?** Evidence: `raster_row_scrolled` blends adjacent resolved scanlines with integer rounding, while `gl_program_init` shifts geometry and casts local coordinates to texel coordinates. Options: evaluate the same two source samples in the shader, or shift/sample a resolved text surface with explicitly equivalent rounding. Hold fixed chrome and RGB regions invariant and compare fractional origins across a row boundary, including the overscan source.

**Behavior and functions.** `render_gl_backend` supplies the adapter. `gl_load`, `gl_load_api`, `gl_context_init`, `gl_init` and `gl_finish_init` dynamically load EGL/GL, match the X visual or Wayland EGL window, require the Law 5 GL 3.3 core context, allocate resources and warm the draw on a worker. `gl_resize`, `gl_submit`, `gl_upload_pending`, `gl_draw` and `gl_present` separate prepaid CPU snapshot from GL upload, retained framebuffer, region composition, fence and swap. **Boundary and defects.** `gl_select_upload` and `gl_init_mode` select experimental direct modes and a legacy expanded-instance path from environment variables. Drop legacy expansion, the variant matrix and the requirement for the app to include GL headers. `gl_storage_available` and the persistent branch of `gl_resources` establish that coherent persistent mapping needs buffer storage beyond the mandatory core baseline. An ordinary compositor discard can thus poison subsequent frames. PLAN §4.6 distinguishes PRESENTED, DISCARDED and LOST. Keep the real fence, retire discarded frames untimed and allow later presentation. Quiescent restart handles actual lost feedback/device state.

**Port witnesses.** `gl_test`, `gl_gate_test`, `gl_bench_test`, `gl_scroll_test`, `wayland_gl_test`, `gl_fuzz` and `gl_bench`. Keep stale/duplicate serials, device loss, warmup failure/cleanup and readback, and add discard followed by a displayed frame. Use the real X11 and Wayland exit cases as well as the mock. A callback-only wake cannot count as a measured display.

**Q7 (§4.6). Which baseline-compatible upload wins on the target displays?** Evidence: `gl_upload_pending` has SUBDATA and ORPHAN paths; `gl_resources` and `gl_context_init` gate PERSISTENT on buffer storage. Options: update the previously allocated buffer, or orphan the store then upload. Include supported persistent mapping as a measured comparator with its capability recorded. Compare identical cell/TBO scenes, padded atlas updates, region-only and paced scroll on both platforms. Record release allocations, input-to-submit tails, fence latency and actual matching presentations, with power/load. No winner follows from this source read.

**Q8 (§4.6). How does discard retire a depth-one frame while its fence is outstanding?** Evidence: GL collapses discard into loss, yet `wayland_frame_discarded` can tell them apart and T5 protects pixels. Options: retain a terminal display result until the independent device signal arrives, or carry separately acknowledged device/display obligations on the frame owner. Check both delivery orders, stale feedback after restart, and the next displayed frame without manufacturing T6.

**Boundary and finding 7.** Do not carry the parallel logical, visual, pending and display top states maintained by `editor_scroll_mirror` and `editor_scroll_follow`. ## layout — unified clip/wrap emission and incremental repair

| Oracle directory under `src/` | File and comment-free lines (M) |
|---|---|
| `layout/` | `checkpoint.c` 133, `layout.c` 570, `layout.h` 158, `wrap.c` 772, `wrap.h` 22 |

**Source footprint (M) 1,655 lines**, the sum of the file counts above.

**Behavior and functions.** `layout_init`, `layout_wrap_init`, `layout_set_wrap`, `layout_begin`, `layout_begin_snapshot` and `layout_begin_visual` bind prepaid storage, source and geometry. `layout_run` and `layout_wrap_run` turn graphemes, tab stops, newlines, gutters, wrapped whitespace, synthetic indentation and wide edges into cells and hit intervals. Preserve MORE with continuation state and no partially certified output. `layout_set_cursor`, `layout_set_cursor_visual`, `layout_set_selection`, `layout_set_paint`, `layout_approximate`, `layout_busy` and `layout_gutter_width/columns` express decorations, affinity and visible geometry. Clip at zero wrap columns and wrap at positive width (G: PLAN §4.5 mode definition). Both modes must apply identical cluster, style and hit rules. `layout_cluster_glyph` is the A16 font/presentation seam.

`layout_edit`, `layout_wrap_edit`, `layout_relayout_rows` and `layout_wrap_relayout` are distinct operations: changed source, or unchanged source with changed paint/caret. For a local wrapped edit, plan the affected logical line, move the valid suffix, repaint changed rows and fill newly exposed bottom rows (`finish_plan`). Newline edits can invalidate to the viewport bottom. Edits before the top shift offsets or invalidate a crossed anchor. A gutter-width change or edit during planning can require restarting. A soft break may have inspected text beyond its displayed end; `lookahead_word_edit` is a guard against narrowing the dependency to the visible byte range. Move/rebuild hit metadata together with cells and row certificates.

`layout_checkpoint_init/request/event/invalidate`, `layout_set_checkpoints` and `checkpoint_worker` give long-line byte/column checkpoints from a snapshot and reject stale publication; edits invalidate from their dependency boundary. A grapheme/column checkpoint alone cannot certify wrap phase for an arbitrary width. `layout_visual_row`, `layout_visual_row_anchored`, `layout_visual_row_fresh`, `layout_wrap_current_rows`, `layout_wrap_clamp_end` and `layout_wrap_follow_edit_anchored` distinguish an exact local row, an approximate deep seed and an in-progress frontier. Keep a local upward row-start cache only. Use `piece_lines_before` / `piece_lines_start` for known rank/start or their unknown result. A19 permits one rank for the first visible row per frame (G, A19 Lines freeze); advance remaining labels from row/newline information.

**Boundary and finding 7.** In `scan_row`, `layout q; memset(&q,0,offsetof(layout,win))` clears the prefix of an entire layout object, including cluster scratch/cache, then constructs a one-row grid and plan. `row_above` can repeat that construction on every predecessor. A limit on *calls* per slice does not bound work per original caret-follow intent. W0.6h timed Ctrl+S to rename after wrapped Select All/Delete of a 33.6 MB buffer: 0.776 s quiet ([bat], load 1.11), 32.562 s with 12 busy CPUs ([bat], load 3.34 → 8.11); the separate DWARF run found 78% of samples under the follow chain ([AC], load 17.26 → 34.06). All are (M), from `docs/decisions/v2-he7.15.md`, W0.6h diagnostic table/profile, summarized as finding 7 in `docs/worklog/2026-11-22.md`. Repetition once per editor slice is that report's unconfirmed hypothesis (E). A11's acceptance counts **rows laid out for the entire operation**, independent of document length. No stopwatch threshold follows from the profile.

Keep the emitter opaque: v1's public `layout` exposes input buffers, phases, raw row arrays, paint callbacks and a mutable render grid. In `wrap.c`, even scratch `wrap_plan[rows-1].next` carries lookahead information. Borrow generation-stamped const cells/hits over prepaid storage; reject stale generations before paint or hit-testing. Delete the separate row-breaking loops and one-row layout reconstruction without losing suffix reuse or checkpoint progress.

**Port witnesses.** `layout_test` (especially `test_dirty`, `test_budgeted_cluster`, `test_cache_invalid_right_context`, `test_long_line_bounded`, `test_checkpoints`, `test_no_allocations`); `wrap_test` (`c26_follow_truth`, `c26_restart_rows`, `c26_reset_rows`, `lookahead_word_edit`, `large_hscroll`, `newline_edits`, `typing_allocations`); `layout_fuzz`, `layout_bench` and the integrated `near_end_repro`/`starved_follows` in `editor_large_test`. Compare incremental cells, hits and affinity against fresh layout. Keep the Wave 2 10 GiB lazy-mapped wrapped case (G, PLAN Wave 2 exit) separate from v1 fixture sizes.

**Q12 (§4.5). Which unchanged boundary permits incremental emission to stop?** Evidence: `finish_plan` shifts a suffix by changed row count and `layout_wrap_edit` tracks lookahead past a rewind; newline, gutter and continuation-indent changes propagate. Options: replan the whole affected logical line before splicing its suffix, or stop on a matched successor certificate plus equivalent break/decoder context. Compare with fresh layout across repeated edits, CRLF, combining clusters, tabs and offscreen word breaks.

**Q13 (§4.5). When may paint and hit-testing borrow a partially recomputed generation?** Evidence: `current_rows` treats a known row start at the frontier differently from a complete row; v1 exposes both through mutable arrays. Options: publish only a completed viewport, or publish a certified prefix with its generation and explicit extent. Compare retained fallback display, pointer hits during MORE and replacement of in-flight checkpoint results. Both require zero per-key copying of the published view (G, PLAN §4.5).
